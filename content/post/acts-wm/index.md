Our paper “[Self-Supervised Latent Action-Conditioned World Modeling for Time Series](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37963)” has been released on arXiv.

We propose ACTS-WM, a self-supervised world modeling framework that learns latent actions to model time-series transitions. Experiments across datasets from different domains show its effectiveness in various downstream tasks.
